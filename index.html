Biography

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. I obtained my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Université Paris Dauphine – PSL, under the supervision of Prof. Agnès Sulem and Prof. Hamed Amini.

Afterward, I was a Postdoctoral Instructor at New York University Shanghai, where I was mentored by Prof. Mathieu Laurière. My research interests include mathematical finance, stochastic analysis, mean field game/control, with a particular focus on complex heterogeneous models and their applications to finance and artificial intelligence.
