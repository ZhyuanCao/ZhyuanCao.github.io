Openings

← Back to homepage

We are looking for motivated candidates to join our group. Applications are welcome from prospective Ph.D. students and postdoctoral researchers interested in stochastic models and mean field analysis in mathematical finance, computational finance and systemic risk, and the intersection of AI and finance.

Ph.D. students

We have a few openings available for graduate students with strong mathematical background. Applicants with research experience will be given preferential consideration.

Funding and benefits

  • Tuition waiver
  • Monthly living stipend of RMB 5,000–7,000

Application materials

  • Curriculum vitae
  • Academic transcripts
  • Relevant research papers, thesis, or other supporting materials

Postdoctoral researchers

Candidates should hold, or be close to completing, a Ph.D. in applied mathematics. Candidates' research interests should align with mathematical finance, uncertainty quantification, or machine learning.

Salary and benefits

  • Eligible postdoctoral fellows receive a pre-tax living allowance of RMB 210,000 per year from provincial and municipal authorities, up to RMB 420,000 in total
  • The university provides a postdoctoral subsidy of RMB 50,000 per year, up to RMB 100,000 in total
  • The supervisor provides an annual salary of at least RMB 60,000; the final amount is negotiable based on the applicant's qualifications and achievements
  • Those eligible for the Guangdong Overseas Postdoctoral Talent Support Program may apply for a pre-tax subsidy of RMB 600,000 over two years and, if they remain in Guangdong, a post-appointment subsidy of RMB 400,000 over three years; this support cannot be combined with the provincial and municipal living allowance
  • Postdoctoral fellows may apply as principal investigators for research funding at all levels
  • Postdoctoral fellows may voluntarily apply for Shenzhen household registration upon appointment
  • Shenzhen provides a living allowance of RMB 360,000 over three years to postdoctoral fellows who remain in or move to Shenzhen after completing their appointment
  • Postdoctoral funding policies are subject to the latest official regulations

Application materials

  • Curriculum vitae
  • A research statement
  • Representative papers or preprints
  • List of publications

How to apply

Send your materials by email

Please email caozhongyuan@cuhk.edu.cn with the subject line “Ph.D. Application – Your Name” or “Postdoctoral Application – Your Name.”
